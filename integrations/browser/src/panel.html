This tab

While mirroring is on, this tab's address, title, readable text, your selection and a screenshot (every few seconds while the page changes, about once a minute while it is still) go to rika! on this computer. rika! keeps only the latest frame, in memory: it is dropped when you switch mirroring off, close the tab, after 2.5 minutes without a frame, or when rika! stops. Only passages you quote into a note are saved.
